# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Reqly is an open project and everyone is welcome to take part: through issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews. We want it to be a friendly, respectful and harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, gender identity, nationality, appearance, religion, disability or any other personal characteristic.

---

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming
- Stay on topic and keep discussions useful for others
- Own your mistakes and learn from them

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory jokes, language or imagery
- Sexualised language or unwelcome advances
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code reviews) and to anyone representing the project in public.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **[hey@tanisheesh.in](mailto:hey@tanisheesh.in)** with the subject `Conduct: Reqly`. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, the maintainer may:

| Level | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. Correction | A private note explaining what was inappropriate |
| 2. Warning | A public or private warning, with limits on further interaction |
| 3. Temporary ban | Temporary removal from all project spaces |
| 4. Permanent ban | Permanent removal from all project spaces |

The maintainer's decision is final. Comments, commits, issues or PRs that violate this code may be edited or removed.
